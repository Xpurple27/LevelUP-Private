# **App Name**: Math Spark

## Core Features:

- Hero Section & Call to Action: A prominent hero section featuring a strong headline ('Private Matematika Mudah Dipahami, Nilai Naik Signifikan'), subheadline, a professional photo of the tutor, and primary calls-to-action like 'Daftar Sekarang' and 'Konsultasi Gratis' to attract immediate attention and drive conversion.
- About the Tutor Profile: A dedicated section detailing the tutor's full name, educational background, teaching experience (bimbel, private, lab assistant), and teaching philosophy emphasizing conceptual understanding and personalization, to build trust.
- Services Overview: A clear presentation of the private math tutoring services offered, including descriptions for different levels (SMP, SMA, UTBK prep), and modalities (online/offline tutoring and specific geographical areas for offline).
- Key Advantages: A bullet-point section highlighting the unique selling points of the tutoring service, such as step-by-step learning methods, concept-focused approach, out-of-class support availability, and customized materials and questions.
- Teaching Activity Gallery: A visual gallery showcasing real teaching moments through photos of in-person sessions, screenshots of online learning, and documentation of student activities, serving as social proof.
- Student Testimonials: A section featuring positive feedback and success stories from past students or parents, including their name, class, and results before and after tutoring, to boost credibility and conversion.
- Real Results Showcase: A section presenting tangible student achievements, such as score improvements (e.g., from 60 to 85), successful UTBK admissions, or increases in academic ranking, to demonstrate impact.
- Pricing & Packages Transparency: A transparent display of tutoring packages and their costs, including details like session duration (e.g., 90 minutes), package discounts (e.g., 8x meetings), and intensive program specifics (e.g., UTBK intensive strategies).
- Conversion Call to Action Section: A dedicated call-to-action section towards the bottom of the page with urgent messaging ('Slot terbatas setiap bulan') and clear buttons like 'Daftar Sekarang' and 'Chat WhatsApp' to drive final conversion.
- Contact Information: A section providing multiple contact methods for prospective students and parents, including direct links to WhatsApp, Instagram profile, and Email, for easy inquiries.
- Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ): A highly recommended section addressing common questions to proactively resolve user concerns, such as online class availability, support for struggling students, and payment systems.
- WhatsApp Call to Action: Direct integration of WhatsApp call-to-action buttons for immediate communication and lead conversion, present in key sections.
- Responsive Mobile Design: Ensuring the landing page is fully responsive and optimized for mobile devices, providing a seamless user experience across all screen sizes and orientations.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A vibrant yet approachable red (#CC1717) to evoke passion and energy for learning, reflecting the user's initial request.
- Background color: A very light, almost white tone (#F9F6F6) to provide a clean and inviting canvas, ensuring high readability and effective contrast.
- Font: A clear and easily readable sans-serif typeface, such as 'Open Sans', ensuring educational content is easily digestible for all users.
- Minimalist, line-art style icons related to mathematics (e.g., geometric shapes, math symbols) and academic achievement (e.g., graduation caps, lightbulbs) will be used to enhance understanding and engagement without overwhelming the clean design.
- A clean, minimal, and mobile-first layout with ample white space, ensuring focus on content and optimal viewing across all device sizes. Sections are clearly delineated with prominent headings.
